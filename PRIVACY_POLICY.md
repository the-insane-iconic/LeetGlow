# Privacy Policy for LeetGlow

*Last updated: November 1, 2026*

LeetGlow ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data for the **LeetGlow** Chrome Extension.

---

### 1. Overview & Single Purpose
LeetGlow is an aesthetic productivity extension built specifically to enhance the user experience on LeetCode (`leetcode.com` and `leetcode.cn`) with customized themes, generative lofi soundscapes, and focus tools.

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or payment credentials.
- **No Browsing History:** We do not monitor, record, or track your browsing activity outside of styling the problem pages on `leetcode.com` and `leetcode.cn`.
- **No Keystroke Tracking:** We do not track, read, log, or transmit any code you write in the LeetCode editor.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** We do not embed Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or any third-party tracking or advertising scripts.

### 3. Local Data Storage
LeetGlow uses Chrome's built-in local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) exclusively on your device to persist your visual preferences:
- Selected theme (e.g., Paper, Sakura Dream, Ocean Deep, Adventure, Pixel Art).
- Audio settings (volume, mute status, preset).
- Pomodoro timer state and focus preferences.
- Solve streak counters.
- Any custom wallpaper you upload (stored locally on your device).

This data never leaves your browser and is deleted if you uninstall the extension.

### 4. Network Communications & Host Permissions
LeetGlow communicates over the network solely for the following explicit functionality:
- **`https://kxtdvzyftfhryvbpbohb.supabase.co/*`**: Used solely to fetch public wallpaper imagery and video loops for the aesthetic themes. No user-identifiable data is transmitted with these read-only asset requests.

### 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user information to outside parties.

### 6. Changes to this Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in this repository with an updated revision date.
