Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 · Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Overview & Data Minimization

LeetGlow ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you install and use the LeetGlow Chrome Extension.

We do not collect, read, store, or transmit your code solutions, LeetCode passwords, keystrokes, or general browsing history.

2. Locally Stored Preferences

Your chosen visual theme, background opacity, blur settings, sound volume, and any custom uploaded wallpapers are saved locally on your device via Chrome's native chrome.storage API. This data stays on your machine and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Voluntary Support Contributions

When you choose to support LeetGlow via our Support page, transactions are processed securely through Razorpay. We collect:

  • Contact Details: Your name and email address to issue a contribution receipt.
  • Transaction Record: Razorpay Payment ID, contribution amount, and transaction timestamp.

We never see or store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking credentials. All payments are processed directly within Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant environment.

4. Permissions & Justifications

  • storage: Saves theme preferences, custom wallpapers, and local display state.
  • sidePanel: Renders the LeetGlow control panel alongside LeetCode problems.
  • offscreen: Enables ambient lofi soundtrack and celebration audio playback under Chrome Manifest V3 regulations.
  • host_permissions (*.supabase.co): Retrieves public curated wallpaper assets from storage buckets.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with enterprise infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Cloud storage for public curated wallpapers and video assets.
